🏆 IMECE India 2026 · National FinalistCase Study 01Aug 2026

Prediction & Control of Welding Distortion in Pressure Vessels

Team Captain · Robo Rebels · BEVPL problem statement · National Finalist (Top 15 / 300+)

MY CONTRIBUTION Led the team workflow, developed the analytical distortion model, worked on the ANSYS thermal-structural validation and integrated the design-report evidence.

The problem

Weld-induced distortion in pressure vessels can appear as shell ovality, seam peaking and local nozzle deformation. The finalized brief covered a 5 mm SS 316L multi-nozzle shell and a 5 mm SA-516 Gr. 70 longitudinal-seam shell, both 1400 mm ID.

The approach

A four-pillar framework combined low heat-input process selection, pre-weld geometric compensation, a parametric Okerblom-type shrinkage model and post-weld dimensional verification.

Simulation & validation

The analytical tool was cross-validated against an ANSYS thermal-structural model on a representative 12 mm, 3,000 mm seam. The baseline comparison reported 80.6% agreement before calibration.

Reported outcome

Heat input was reduced from 1.41 to 0.96 kJ/mm. The report predicts 31.8% lower transverse shrinkage from heat-input optimization and 86.8% lower net residual shrinkage when pre-weld compensation is added. These are model predictions, not shop-floor measurements.

🛡️ National Student Hack-A-Stage 2026Case Study 022026

SafeSense 3D — Uncertainty-Aware Safety for Automated Machinery

Robo Rebels · Track 01 · Algorithmic Safety & Hazard Mitigation

MY CONTRIBUTION Developed the deterministic simulation logic and adaptive safety-envelope workflow used to connect speed, confidence, stopping distance and risk state.

The problem

A fixed safety zone does not adapt to machine speed or sensing uncertainty. The design question was: how much safety space does the machine need right now?

The approach

Sense → Estimate → Adapt → Decide → Act. Virtual LiDAR and thermal cues feed distance, velocity and confidence estimates; the safety envelope then combines stopping distance, reaction distance and uncertainty margin.

Safety principle

Uncertainty can expand the protective envelope, but it cannot reduce the deterministic safety floor. Risk states use distance, time-to-collision, machine state and confidence so each action has a physical reason.

Simulation

The prototype evaluates clear, approach, critical, occlusion, conflicting-sensor and imminent-hazard scenarios. The submission explicitly describes this as deterministic physics simulation with sensor-confidence scenarios, not physical sensor data.

📄 GSoC 2026 ProposalCase Study 042026

Agentic Dora — Safe Agent-Driven Robot Control

Proposal + working prototype for the dora-rs dataflow framework

The problem

LLM agents can generate invalid or unsafe robot commands, so safety validation should not depend on the agent behaving perfectly.

Architecture

Input → agent → action mapping → independent Safety Node → PyBullet simulation, with Dora nodes passing typed JSON messages.

Safety & observability

The Safety Node runs independently, clamps or stops invalid commands and holds the last safe command while a slower LLM call completes. OTLP traces expose decisions to SigNoz.

Outcome

The prototype runs locally; the SigNoz / OTLP observability guide and a Python documentation fix were merged into dora-rs, while the complete proposal was submitted to GSoC 2026.

💡 IMECE 2026 · Innovation SquareCase Study 052026

Smart Hybrid Die Repair

Student ideathon concept · TRL below 4

The problem

Localized die damage can force replacement of an otherwise usable tool. Powder-fed DED can repair the region but brings high equipment cost and leaves a surface that needs finishing.

The concept

A localized repair chain: scan → wire-fed DED → laser surface remelting → in-situ micro-milling → verification.

Integration

Wire feedstock is positioned as a lower-cost feed route; laser remelting conditions the deposited surface; micro-milling completes the repair without moving the part to another machine.

Status

The reported waste-reduction figure is a concept projection. This is an ideation-stage proposal and no physical prototype is claimed.
